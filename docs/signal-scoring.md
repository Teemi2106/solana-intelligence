# Signal scoring

Signal score is an internal 0–100 ranking of observable conditions, not a probability of profit or a safety claim. Each signal keeps its scoring version, component values, raw inputs, contributing wallet versions, independence clusters, detection market snapshot, risk snapshot, and timestamp.

Planned components are wallet quality, wallet independence, convergence strength, relative purchase size, liquidity quality, holder distribution, entry freshness, observable token risk, market conditions, and data confidence. Components degrade when facts are stale or missing; missing evidence is never silently converted into a favorable score.

`EARLY_MOVEMENT`, `SMART_MONEY_CONVERGENCE`, and `EARLY_MOVEMENT_WITH_PUBLIC_CONFIRMATION` remain distinct research cohorts.
